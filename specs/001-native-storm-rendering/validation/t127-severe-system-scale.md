## Evidence and governing ratios

The present representative group spans about Y 222..502 (280 blocks) and its full lateral
descriptor envelope is only about 500 blocks. At a 600-block observation distance that occupies
roughly 45 degrees in its widest view, so it reads as an isolated cloud rather than a horizon-scale
severe weather system.

The target is derived from the required perceptual relationship, not a uniform descriptor multiplier:

- a mature system must cover a horizon-scale field at 600 blocks and remain clearly system-scale at
  900 and 1,200 blocks;
- the base must remain at least three convective-core widths, so it reads as one lower system;
- the tower must remain a substantial vertical column rather than a narrow noise carrier;
- the anvil must exceed tower width by at least 3.5x and feed laterally from its upper third; and
- descriptor count stays ten. Individual lobe allocation therefore has to be role-specific; simply
  scaling every descriptor would leave insufficient base and anvil distribution.
